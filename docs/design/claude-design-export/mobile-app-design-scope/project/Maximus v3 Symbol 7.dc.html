The arms of Maximus
A crown over a shield with mantling either side and a motto ribbon below. The shield is divided in two (per pale). Left, shaded in engraver’s lines: an open door with the sun rising inside it. Right: the five stars of Cassiopeia, joined to make an M. Motto: Historia docet, history teaches.
