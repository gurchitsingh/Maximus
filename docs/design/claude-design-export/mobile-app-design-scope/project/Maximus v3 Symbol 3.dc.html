Maximus v3 · Symbol round 3
Five marks with an idea
Each one is a single geometric shape that carries a letter inside it and says something about history. No decoration, one colour of bronze (two tones where noted), built on a 100-unit grid so it stays sharp from 24 px to a store listing.
My pick is 1, Two arches: the simplest, the clearest meaning, and the only one that reads as itself at every size.

1 · Bridges across time
Two arches
Two arches side by side are the humps of an m. An arch is the oldest structure that still carries weight: Roman aqueducts, Gothic doorways, railway viaducts. The mark says history is what crosses over.
