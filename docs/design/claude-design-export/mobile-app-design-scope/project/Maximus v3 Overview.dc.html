Maximus v3
Frontend overview
A streaming-style Android app for learning history: 20 topics as posters, a study list, short lessons, a locked chat, gentle quizzes and a vertical feed. Dark first, with a light theme. The split-disc mark is on the splash, the onboarding seal and the Home header.

4 · Behaviour
Onboarding gating
Sign in and create account are mock screens. The interests screen needs 3 topics before Next. Skip goes straight to Home and adds the Roman Empire to the study list.
Rating
Thumbs up on a topic raises the match % of other topics in the same group by 4; thumbs down lowers it by 6. The Library posters update behind the sheet.
Study list
Add or remove from the sheet, the match cards or the Study tab. Empty state links to the Library.
Chat
Locked to the open topic. Off-topic questions get "I only know about the Roman Empire." Resting and offline states are designed.
Quiz
Six questions, explanation after each answer, a result with the questions missed. No timer.
Theme and text size
Dark or light, three text sizes, applied to the reader and sample text. Both are set again in Settings.
Read aloud
The bar and speed setting are designed; the audio itself is not built in this prototype.
Clear my data
Resets topics, ratings and picks and returns to the splash.
5 · Build notes
Illustrations
Flat SVG stand-ins in one style: four detailed hero posters (Roman, French Revolution, WW2, Egypt) and 16 simpler motifs. Replace with final art at 2:3.
Content
Only the Roman Empire has lessons, chat and a quiz. The other 19 topics open the title sheet and say lessons are in the full app.
Not built
Real authentication, audio, real chat replies, search, notifications. Chat answers are canned.
Fonts
Figtree, Cinzel, Caveat Brush, Big Shoulders Stencil Display, all on Google Fonts and free to bundle.
Platform
Designed for Android at 412 x 812 dp inside the status and gesture bars.
