4 · Illustration and title lettering
One style across every topic: flat shapes, one dominant silhouette, a light source that becomes the focal circle or flag, three to five colours per poster. No outlines, no gradients inside the art, no photographic texture. The only gradient is a dark scrim under the title so text always passes 4.5:1. These are SVG stand-ins and can be replaced with final artwork at the same 2:3 size.

6 · Motion
Soft, quick, never blocking. Reduced motion replaces every move with a fade.
Poster row parallax
Artwork shifts 6% against the card as the row scrolls, 0 delay.
Added pop
Button fills with bronze, icon swaps to a tick, scale 1 to 1.08 to 1 in 220 ms.
Match % count-up
0 to the value over 700 ms, ease-out, once per appearance.
Tile pop
Selected topic tile scales to 1.06 and settles, a tick fades in, 180 ms.
Answer bounce
Right: a small upward bounce and a tick. Wrong: a 6 px side nudge and a cross. No sound by default.
Finish celebration
Thirty bronze and poster-tint pieces drift down for 1.6 s, then stop. No counters.
7 · Style summary and tokens
Dark-first. Posters supply colour, the interface supplies warmth. Rewards are gentle: no streak loss, no lives, no timers. Overlay text sits on a scrim. Right and wrong carry an icon and a word.
