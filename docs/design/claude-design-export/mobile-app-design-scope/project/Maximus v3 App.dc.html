Why the Republic fell
Armies with their own loyalty
For centuries, Roman soldiers were citizens who went home after a war. That changed when generals began to pay and reward their own troops.
Caesar crosses the line
In 49 BCE Julius Caesar led his army over the Rubicon. Doing that was treason. He won the civil war that followed.
One man in charge
After Caesar was killed in 44 BCE, another civil war began. Octavian won it and was given the name Augustus. The Republic was over, though the Senate still met.
