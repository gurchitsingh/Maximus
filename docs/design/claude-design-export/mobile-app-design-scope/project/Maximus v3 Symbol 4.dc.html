Maximus v3 · Symbol round 4
Four new marks
The arches mark is dropped. Each of these hides an M inside a shape that means something, and none borrows from a known brand mark.
My pick is 1, Cassiopeia: a real constellation, an M the sky already draws, and it feels like curiosity and night-time cinema at once.

Five stars joined into an M: the constellation Cassiopeia, named by the Greeks and used by sailors and storytellers for thousands of years. The letter is the shape the stars already make, and the star in the middle is the biggest, like a sun at the centre of the story.
