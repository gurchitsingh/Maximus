Which topics are paired

Same drawing, day or dusk light: Ancient Greece, Ancient India, Persian Empire, Vikings, Mongol Empire, Renaissance, Ottoman Empire, Mughal Empire, French Revolution, Industrial Revolution, American Revolution, Russian Revolution, Indian independence, British Empire, Crusades, Byzantine Empire, Maya, Aztec, Inca, Mali, Ming China, Samurai Japan, Zulu Kingdom, Napoleon (portrait-style, lighting only).

A compact card on the Home dashboard, above On This Day, with a thumbnail, a date and one fact under 25 words. It is picked from a hand-written list by date, so it works offline. Three days shown.
