2 · Shortlist of three
Door
+ Reads in a blink, and the arch is a Roman form without any lettering.
- Door icons are common; the sun is what makes it ours.
Portal
+ Strong silhouette; rings of time fit a history app.
- Can look like a rainbow or a signal icon out of context.
Coin fragment
+ Most on-theme: a coin with a missing piece suggests something uncovered.
- At small sizes it can read as a pie chart or a loading ring.
Dropped: Path and Question curl lose their detail at 24 px; Eye is too detailed; Horizon and Viewfinder are clear but generic.
The pick
Door
A doorway with a rising sun inside. Door and horizon are both metaphors for starting a story, the arch is Roman without using letters, and the cut-out opening stays open at 24 px. It is also the shape of the bottom of each poster's Start action: always an opening, never a wall.
