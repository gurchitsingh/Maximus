# Claude Design: logo prompt (paste everything below)

You are a senior brand identity designer. Design the logo for an Android app. Work like a logo designer: strategy first, then varied concepts, then tests. Do not give me clip-art.

## The product
A history study app that feels like Netflix, Duolingo and TikTok instead of a textbook. Pick from 28 topics (Rome, Ancient Egypt, the French Revolution, WW2, Sikh history, Maratha Empire, the space race, the Great Depression...), study short lessons, take gentle quizzes, scroll a feed of facts, chat with a topic-only assistant, and a section called "Think Like..." about great kings, queens and philosophers. Audience: curious students and adults, mostly in India, who want something better than doomscrolling. Mood: cinematic, curious, confident, a little playful, never stuffy or newsy.

## Constraints
- The app name is NOT final, so the logo must be a **symbol with no letters and no text**. It must work with any name.
- Colour: warm bronze/gold (about #C8933A) on near-black (#0F0D0A) is the home palette. Show it there, then also in one flat colour.
- A hint of the ancient world is welcome (carved, coin, seal, inscription feel) but it must not look like a museum, a bank or a Roman restaurant. It must feel modern and alive.
- It must read at **24 px**, as an Android adaptive icon (circle and squircle masks, centred in the safe zone), and as a one-colour mark.
- Flat vector only: no gradients, glow, 3D, drop shadows or photo effects.

## What the mark should express
Primary: **curiosity and learning** (the pull to find out the next thing). Secondary: leadership and "greatness" without a literal crown.

## Banned (the cliches I already rejected)
Open book with a spark, lightbulb, graduation cap, laurel wreath, column, eagle, helmet, hourglass, globe, scroll with a star, a generic "M", the Netflix N, the Duolingo owl, anything that looks like stock icons.

## Process (show your work briefly)
1. **Strategy:** in 5 lines: the one idea the mark must carry, three metaphors you rejected and why, three you will explore.
2. **Concepts:** give me **8 distinctly different directions**, each from a different metaphor (examples to inspire, not to copy: a door or keyhole opening onto something; a path or spiral that pulls you in; a fragment of a coin or seal that completes a shape; a horizon or sunrise with a doorway; negative space that hides a second image; a mask or profile; a stairway; a compass or star made from simple geometry). One line of rationale each. Draw all 8 as clean vector marks, large, on the dark background.
3. **Shortlist:** pick your best 3 and say why. Redraw them refined on dark and light backgrounds, in one flat colour, at 48 px and 24 px, in the circle and squircle adaptive-icon masks.
4. **Critique:** for each of the 3, one honest weakness, and what would make it look generic. Fix it.
5. **Final:** your single recommendation, as clean vector artwork I can hand to a developer, with the monochrome version and a 512 px store icon.

## Quality bar
Memorable in one glance. Distinct at 24 px. Ownable (someone could not mistake it for an existing app). Warm, curious, a little surprising.
