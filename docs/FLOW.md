# FLOW: screens and what each button does (v2)

No accounts. Bottom nav: **Home**, **Library**, **Feed**, **Study**, with a **Chat** button that appears only inside an open title.

## 1. Splash
Logo (Roman mark) animates, 1.5 s, then onboarding (first run) or Home.

## 2. Onboarding (first run only, ~60 s, skippable)
Surprise-driven, one idea per screen, illustrated, big motion. Five screens.
1. **Guess the year**: striking illustration; tap one of three years; reveal with a fact and playful reaction.
2. **Mystery card**: tap a sealed card; it flips to reveal a surprising fact.
3. **Pick what pulls you**: illustrated tiles (not text chips) for the topics (and a few people); tap 3 or more; each tile pops. Seeds match %.
4. **Your first matches**: 3 posters with match % counting up, plus "Add to study list".
5. **Theme and text size**: dark (default) / light, one slider. "Start exploring" goes to Home.

## 3. Home (dashboard)
- Greeting + continue-studying card (resume last lesson).
- Study list progress (small rows with bars).
- Quiz scores row (once quizzes exist).
- "For you" row: top match % titles you have not added.
- On This Day card (live, daily).
Tap anything: opens the title or lesson.

## 4. Library
Netflix-style: hero title at top, then horizontal rows (For you, Ancient and medieval, India, Revolutions and empires, Wars and space, Money and crashes, Because you liked X) and a **Think Like...** row of portrait posters (people).
Poster: topic picture + title in that topic's own style + match % badge.
Tap poster: **Title sheet**.

### Title sheet
Big picture, styled title, 2 to 3 line brief, match %, lesson count and read time, rate control (thumbs), **Add to study list** (toggles to "Added"), **Start / Continue**.
Rate: tap thumb, instant, updates match % across the app.

### Person sheet (Think Like... titles)
Portrait illustration, name in its own lettering, era and place, one-line headline, the 3 to 5 traits as tappable cards, "What they changed" and (leaders) "Odds they beat", or (thinkers) core ideas and "Use it today". Same match %, rating and Add to study list as topics.

## 5. Study (study list)
List of added titles with progress. Swipe to remove. Tap opens Reader at the next unfinished lesson.

## 6. Reader
Lesson text, images with credit, read-aloud, previous/next lesson, progress bar. Finish all lessons: celebrate (confetti, gentle), offer the quiz.
Bottom bar: Lessons (list), **Chat**, Quiz.

## 7. Chat (inside a title)
Chat bubbles, suggested questions ("Why did the Republic fall?"). Header shows the title it is locked to. Off-topic: "I only know about <title>." Answers show a small "From lesson 4" tag that jumps to the lesson. Daily cap reached: "Chat is resting, back tomorrow." Offline: disabled with message.

## 8. Quiz
Per title. 6 random questions from the bank. One question per screen, big option buttons, instant right/wrong with a one-line explanation, summary at the end with score and "Review lessons you missed". No timer, no streak.

## 9. Feed
Full-screen vertical cards: image, one short fact, title tag. Swipe up: next. Buttons: Go deeper (opens lesson), Save, thumbs. Only topics on your study list (plus a few "try this" cards from high-match titles).

## 10. Settings
Theme, text size, voice and speed, clear my data (resets ratings/study list), about, image and text credits (CC BY-SA 4.0 notice), version.

## 11. V2: Perspective mode (inside a title)
Pick country and period, then lens (rulers, middle/working, poor). Shows that group's story with an "interpretation" label.

## Rules
- Every screen works offline except Chat and On This Day.
- No sign-in, no notifications in V1.
- Empty states always have a picture and one action.
