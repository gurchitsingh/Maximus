Design a logo for an Android history-study app. Act as a senior brand designer: strategy first, then varied concepts, then tests.

Product: a Netflix-style library of 26 history topics (Rome, Ancient Egypt, French Revolution, WW2, Sikh history, Maratha Empire, space race), short lessons, gentle Duolingo-style quizzes, a TikTok-style fact feed, and a "Think Like a Leader" section on great kings and queens. Mood: cinematic, curious, confident, slightly playful. Never stuffy, newsy or museum-like.

Constraints:
- Symbol only: no letters or text, because the app name is not final.
- Palette: bronze #C8933A on near-black #0F0D0A, plus a one-flat-colour version.
- Flat vector: no gradients, glow, 3D or shadows.
- Must read at 24 px and work as an Android adaptive icon (circle and squircle masks).
- A faint ancient feel (carved, coin, seal) is welcome, but it must look modern and alive.

The mark should express the pull of curiosity, the urge to find out what comes next. Secondary: greatness, without a literal crown.

Banned clichés: open book, spark, lightbulb, graduation cap, laurel, column, eagle, helmet, hourglass, globe, scroll, generic M, Netflix N, Duolingo owl, stock icons.

Process:
1. Strategy in five lines: the one idea, three metaphors rejected and why, three explored.
2. Eight clearly different concepts from different metaphors (a doorway opening, a path pulling you in, a coin fragment completing a shape, negative space hiding a second image, a stairway, a compass made of simple geometry). One line of rationale each.
3. Shortlist three. Show them on dark and light, in one colour, at 48 and 24 px, in both icon masks.
4. Give one honest weakness for each, then fix it.
5. Final pick as clean vector, with a monochrome version and a 512 px store icon.

Quality bar: memorable in one glance, distinct at 24 px, ownable, warm and a little surprising.
