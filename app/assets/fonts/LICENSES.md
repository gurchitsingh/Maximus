# Bundled fonts

All four families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Each folder holds the
licence text (`OFL.txt`) next to the font files. The app registers every `OFL.txt` with
Flutter's `LicenseRegistry`, so they appear on the licences page.

| Family | Designer | Weights | Folder | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Figtree | Erik Kennedy | 500, 600, 700, 800, 900 | `Figtree/` | https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Figtree |
| Cinzel | Natanael Gama | 700 | `Cinzel/` | https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cinzel |
| Caveat Brush | Impallari Type | 400 | `CaveatBrush/` | https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Caveat+Brush |
| Big Shoulders Stencil Display | Patric King | 900 | `BigShouldersStencil/` | https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Big+Shoulders+Stencil+Display |

The files are the static instances served by Google Fonts and are unmodified. Under the OFL,
the fonts may be bundled in the app, but the font files may not be sold on their own.
