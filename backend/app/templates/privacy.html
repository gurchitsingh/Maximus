Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

Maximus is a history learning app. This page says what we keep about you, why, for how long, and how to remove it.

What we keep

  • Your account: your email address, a display name if you give one, and either a password (stored only as a one-way hash) or the id Google gives us when you sign in with Google.
  • Your study data: the topics and leaders you picked, your study list, ratings, lesson progress, quiz scores, saved and liked feed cards, and app settings such as theme and text size.
  • Your chats: the questions you ask the chat inside a title and its answers.

We do not keep your location, contacts, photos or device identifiers. There are no ads and no analytics or tracking tools in the app.

Why we keep it

To sign you in, to keep your progress safe if you lose your phone, to show the same data on all your devices, and to let you read back earlier chats. Daily limits on chat are counted per account so the free service stays available to everyone.

How long we keep it

  • Chats: $retention days, then they are deleted automatically. You can delete one title's chat or all chats at any time in the app.
  • Everything else: until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Our server logs record errors and timings, not your email address or what you wrote.

Who processes it for us

  • Render runs the app's server.
  • Neon hosts the database.
  • Our email provider sends sign-in and account emails.
  • Google handles "Continue with Google" if you use it.
  • OpenRouter and Google Gemini receive the text of your chat question, the title's lessons and your last few chat turns, only to write the answer.

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings, then Delete my account. Without the app: use the account deletion page; we email you a link to confirm. Deleting your account removes your account, study data and chats from our database straight away. We keep no copy.
